Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about borrowing with Pesa Credit in Uganda.

What documents are required to apply for a loan?
You need a valid Ugandan National ID (NIN), an active phone number registered with Mobile Money (MTN or Airtel), and proof of income or business location in Uganda.
What are your interest rates and repayment periods?
Our interest rates start at a competitive 5% per annum. Repayment terms range flexibly from 1 month to 24 months based on your loan agreement.
How fast is the loan approval and disbursement?
Once your documentation is verified, loans are typically approved within 15 to 30 minutes. Funds are sent straight to your Mobile Money account or bank account within 24 hours.
Do I need physical land titles or heavy collateral?
No! For micro and business loans, we accept personal guarantors, mobile money cashflow statements, or movable business assets.
How do I make my monthly loan repayments?
Repayments can be made via Mobile Money merchant codes, bank transfer, or directly at our office in Kampala.
